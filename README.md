# Oculo 👁️

### AI-Powered Screen Analysis Assistant

**See your screen. Understand what matters. Get AI-powered assistance.**

**Oculo** is an AI-powered screen analysis assistant that lets you select a specific area of your screen and use AI to understand, analyze, and assist with the content inside that area.

The idea behind Oculo is simple: instead of explaining what's on your screen manually, **select it and let AI understand it.**

---

## ✨ Features

### 👁️ Select Any Area

Select exactly the portion of your screen you want Oculo to analyze.

The selection interface is designed around a simple **four-corner selection frame**, allowing you to precisely choose the visual area you want to send for analysis.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Analysis

Once an area is selected, Oculo analyzes the captured content using AI and provides useful information based on what is visible.

It can help understand:

* 📝 Text and written content
* 🖼️ Images and visual information
* 🖥️ Applications and interfaces
* 📊 Information displayed on screen
* 🔍 Specific visual details
* 💡 General questions about selected content

### 🎯 Precise Selection

Oculo focuses only on the area you select.

This makes it possible to analyze a specific part of your screen instead of unnecessarily processing the entire display.

### ✨ Analyze Area

After selecting an area, the **Analyze Area** action lets you send the selected content to AI for analysis.

The interface provides a clear visual indication of the action so the workflow remains simple:

**Select → Analyze → Understand**

### 💬 AI Assistance

After analyzing the selected area, Oculo can provide contextual assistance based on what was captured.

This makes it useful for understanding unfamiliar content, reading information, interpreting visual elements, or asking questions about something currently visible on your screen.

---

## 🖥️ How Oculo Works

### 1. Select

Open Oculo and select the exact area of the screen you want to analyze.

### 2. Analyze

Press **Analyze Area** to send the selected content for AI processing.

### 3. Understand

Oculo analyzes the selected content and provides an AI-generated response.

### 4. Assist

Use the resulting information to understand or work with what's on your screen.

## 🔐 Privacy & Selection

Oculo is designed around **user-controlled screen selection**.

Instead of automatically analyzing everything on the screen, the user chooses the specific area they want to analyze.

This gives you more control over what visual information is processed.

## ⚡ Why Oculo?

Traditional AI assistants often require you to:

1. Look at something on your screen
2. Explain what you're seeing
3. Type or describe the relevant information
4. Ask the AI a question

Oculo simplifies that process.

> **If you can see it, select it. Let Oculo understand it.**
